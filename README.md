# A-Statistical-Study-on-Annual-Market-Price-Production-of-Egg-Milk-and-Meat-in-Kerala-using-R
This project presents a comprehensive statistical analysis of the annual market price and production of eggs, milk, and meat in the state of Kerala, along with the study of influencing factors.  The study was conducted as part of an MSc Statistics (Data Analytics Specialization) internship at the Directorate of Animal Husbandry, Trivandrum
The project applies Time Series Analysis, ARIMA Modeling, Regression Analysis, and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to understand trends, forecast future prices/production, and analyze contributing factors.

# Objectives

Analyze price trends of eggs, milk (cow, buffalo, goat), and meat (1999–2022)

Perform Time Series Analysis and fit ARIMA models

Conduct Regression Analysis for production factors

Forecast future prices and production trends

# Tools Used

R (ARIMA, ACF, Forecasting)

Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Tabulation)

# Results

Strong upward trend in egg, milk, and meat prices.

Best-fit ARIMA models used for forecasting.

Regression models identified key production influences.

Forecast indicates continued price growth in coming years.
